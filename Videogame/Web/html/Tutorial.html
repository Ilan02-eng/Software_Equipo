Story

You are a small black cat who just arrived in the quiet village of Finetown in search of a new beginning in your new home. The village appears peaceful, colorful, and welcoming to your arrival, fulfilled with friendly faces, cozy homes, and winding paths waiting to be explored.

Yet beneath its charm, something feels out of place.

As the days pass, you begin to notice strange details that nobody seems willing to explain. Certain houses remain locked at unusual hours. The villagers avoid discussing what happens after sunset. Mysterious sounds echo through the streets during the night, and some mornings people seem unable to remember what happened the evening before. Curiosity soon leads you beyond the safety of your new home. By exploring the village, speaking with its residents, and searching hidden corners for clues, you will slowly uncover secrets buried deep within Finetown's history. Every discovery raises new questions, and every night seems to bring the village one step closer to revealing its true nature.

Welcome Letter

Welcome to the small Finetown!
We are the town where everything is fine. We would be thankful that since your arrival, lets follow a couple of rules:

1. Feel free to explore your surroundings. Nature is full of fruits and surprises.

2. Please return home before nightfall. Nights can be unpredictable.

3. Be nice to everyone. A small act of kindness may be remembered longer than you think.

4. If you notice unusual behaviour, do not panic. It will return to normal in the morning.

5. If you hear something calling your name in the night... ignore it.

6. Do not enter locked houses. Some doors are closed for a reason.

We hope you enjoy your stay. You'll get used to it.

Objective

Your journey is about more than simple survival. As you explore Finetown, you will discover unusual cards, hidden rooms, forgotten stories, and mysterious encounters that challenge both your strategy and your courage. Each expedition into a house presents new opportunities and dangers. Search carefully for useful resources, build a powerful collection of cards, and learn how to adapt to unexpected situations. The choices you make during each run will influence how prepared you are for future encounters. As more secrets come to light, you will gradually piece together the truth behind the strange events occurring throughout the village and uncover what lies at the heart of Finetown's mystery.

Gameplay

Exploration plays a central role in the game. Every house contains multiple rooms, but their arrangement changes from run to run. Some rooms may contain useful cards, resources, or fragments of information, while others may hide unexpected encounters. Because the layout is randomized, no two adventures are exactly alike. A room that was safe during one run may contain a challenge during the next. Careful exploration is often rewarded, but curiosity can also lead to danger. As you progress, you will collect new cards that expand your options during encounters. Building a balanced deck and learning how different card types interact is essential for success. Each run provides an opportunity to experiment with new strategies, discover hidden combinations, and improve your understanding of the village's mysteries.
