Tutorial

Story

You will be taking the role of a small black cat who arrives in the quiet village of Finetown in search of a new beginning and a new life. At first glance, the town seems welcoming and peaceful, a landscape surrounded by fruits and flowers where everything is full of life.

Yet something feels unusual.

Strange noises can be heard after sunset, some doors remain locked, and the villagers seem unwilling to discuss what happens at night. As you explore your surroundings, you will slowly uncover the secrets hidden beneath the village's cheerful appearance.

Welcome Letter

Feel free to explore your surroundings. Nature is full of fruits and surprises.

Please return home before nightfall. Nights can be unpredictable.

Be nice to everyone. A small act of kindness may be remembered longer than you think.

If you notice unusual behaviour, do not panic. It will return to normal in the morning.

If you hear something calling your name in the night... ignore it.

Do not enter locked houses. Some doors are closed for a reason.

We hope you enjoy your stay. You'll get used to it.

Objective

Explore the village, discover hidden cards, survive dangerous encounters and uncover the truth behind the strange events taking place in Finetown.

Gameplay

Every house contains surprises. Some rooms may hide useful rewards, while others may contain dangerous encounters. The layout of houses, available cards and encounters change every run, ensuring that no two adventures are exactly the same. Explore carefully, collect cards and prepare yourself for whatever lies behind the next door.

Controls

  • W or ↑ - Move Up
  • A or ← - Move Left
  • S or ↓ - Move Down
  • D or → - Move Right
  • Hover Mouse - View card information
  • Left Click - Play selected card

Card Types

Attack Cards

Offensive cards used to confront threats.

Defense Cards

Protect yourself from incoming attacks.

Healing Cards

Restore health and provide support.

Control Cards

Influence enemies and alter the flow of combat.
